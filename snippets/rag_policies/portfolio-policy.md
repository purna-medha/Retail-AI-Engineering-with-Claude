# Classroom portfolio policy

## Concentration limit

No single asset may exceed 35% of total portfolio value in this simulation.
Concentration is the value of an asset divided by the value of the whole
portfolio. Check the ratio using the portfolio that would result from the
proposed trade. A position at exactly 35% is within the limit. A position
above the limit is flagged for manual review.

## Review record

The reviewer records the proposed asset, the calculated share, and the
reason for the decision. Capture any questions about missing prices before
approving the review. Keep the approved calculation with the decision so
that another reviewer can check the evidence later.
